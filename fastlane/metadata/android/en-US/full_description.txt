<p>NNA Clicker is a touch recorder and auto clicker for Android. Record taps, swipes and multi-finger gestures once, then replay them with precise timing. No root is required.</p>

<p><b>Recording and playback</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Taps, swipes, holds and multi-finger gestures (up to 10 pointers)</li>
<li>Repeat count or infinite loop, speed from 0.5× to 3×, delays and auto stop</li>
<li>Optional randomization of touch position and timing</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Auto tap</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Quick auto tap: tap one spot and it repeats</li>
<li>Multi-target auto tap with per-target delay, and saved setups you can rename and reuse</li>
<li>A Running pill with a Stop button while it plays; Volume Down always stops</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Image steps and flow</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Wait for an image, tap it wherever it appears, or wait until it disappears</li>
<li>Labels, Go to, if / else on images, random waits, Back / Home / Open app steps</li>
<li>Run log saves the screen when an image step fails, with one-tap fixes</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Two input engines</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Standard: uses the accessibility service</li>
<li>Shizuku (optional): real-time recording and millisecond-accurate playback</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>The app has no internet permission. It uses the Accessibility API only to read touches while you record and to replay your gestures. Screenshots for image steps are matched on the device and never leave it. There are no ads or trackers.</p>
